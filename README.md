## 🏛️ Flagship HADP (Holistic Agriculture Development Program) Integrations

Aligned with the Government of Jammu & Kashmir's flagship **HADP initiative** (Agriculture Production Department & Sheep Husbandry Department) and **SKUAST-Kashmir**:

### 1. 💰 Project 24: Self-Sufficiency in Mutton Production in J&K
* **Bridging the Deficit:** J&K imports over ₹1,400+ Crore of live sheep/mutton annually. HADP Project 24 incentivizes local sheep farming to achieve regional self-sufficiency.
* **Commercial Sheep/Goat Farms (50-Ewe Units):** 50% DBT Capital Subsidy (up to ₹2,50,000 government grant) for educated rural youth, women SHGs, and progressive pastoralists.
* **High-Altitude *Kotha/Dhoka* Infrastructure:** Up to ₹1,00,000 subsidy for predator-proof chainlink night shelters and paddocks at highland alpine pastures (*Bahaks*).
* **Fodder Bunkers & Silage Pits:** 50% subsidy (up to ₹30,000) for high-density vacuum-wrapped silage storage to sustain flocks during snowbound winters.

### 2. ✂️ Project 27: Promotion of Wool/Pelt Processing & Marketing
* **Mechanical Shearing Modernization:** 50% subsidy vouchers on professional 6-speed electric shearing clipper kits to replace traditional hand shears, eliminate skin injuries, and preserve staple wool length.
* **Wool Quality Grading & Laboratory Testing:** Fine fleece certification against Kashmir Merino breed standards (19.0 – 21.5 microns, 75mm staple length, <2% Vegetable Matter).

### 3. 🧮 Interactive DBT Subsidy Calculator & Kisan Sathi Pre-Screening
* **Real-Time Subsidy Calculator:** Computes scheme capital cost, 50% government grant, 10% farmer margin equity, and bank/KCC term loan financing.
* **Kisan Sathi Pre-Application Pass:** Instant submission generates an official acknowledgment slip with reference ID (`HADP-JK-SHP-XXXXX`) for verification at the nearest **Kisan Khidmat Ghar (KKG)** or District Sheep Husbandry Office (DSHO).

### 4. 🎓 Daksh Kisan LMS & SKUAST-Kashmir Digital Certifications
* Integrated access to free skill training modules:
  1. *Self-Sufficiency in Mutton Production* (Breeding, Lamb Brooding & Barn Design)
  2. *Modern Wool Harvesting & Clipper Maintenance*
  3. *Scientific Winter Feeding & Silage Fermentation in Sub-Zero Climates*

## 🔬 Veterinary Science & Digital Animal Welfare

### 1. 📋 Bharat Pashudhan (INAPH) Digital Livestock Health Passport
* **12-Digit Unique RFID Ear Tag:** Every live sheep listing features an authentic INAPH tag (e.g. `1901-4455-6677`) with a simulated barcode graphic.
* **Prophylactic Vaccination Dossier:**
  - Enterotoxaemia (ETV) vaccination & annual booster tracking
  - Sheep Pox prophylaxis (Kashmir strain)
  - PPR (Peste des Petits Ruminants) live attenuated vaccine
  - Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD) tetravalent protection
* **Parasite Prophylaxis Logs:** Anthelmintic dosing (Fenbendazole / Oxyclozanide against Liver Fluke) and chemical dipping vat records (Deltamethrin 1% against scabies, ticks, and mites).
* **Attesting Officer:** Digital seal, registration number, and signature from the local Veterinary Assistant Surgeon (VAS).

### 2. ❄️ SKUAST-K Scientific Winter Feeding Planner (Chillai Kalan Strategy)
* Interactive ration calculator based on flock size (5 to 1,000 head), physiological status (maintenance, pregnant, lactating), and winter severity.
* Calculates daily requirement of:
  - Meadow Hay (*Gaas*)
  - High-Density Fermented Maize Silage (pH 3.8 – 4.2, 8.8% Crude Protein)
  - SKUAST-K Balanced Concentrate Mixture (Maize 40%, Mustard Cake 30%, Wheat Bran 28%, Mineral Salt 2%)
  - Urea Molasses Mineral Block (UMMB) licks & willow tree foliage (*Vier pan*)

### 3. 🏔️ Highland Alpine Pasture (*Bahak*) Migration & Transhumance Tracker
* Real-time waypoints, checkpoints, and emergency welfare facilities across key migratory corridors:
  - **Route 1:** Pir Panjal / Mughal Road Corridor (*Shopian ➔ Dubjan ➔ Peer Ki Gali ➔ Bafliaz/Rajouri*)
  - **Route 2:** Sonamarg – Baltal – Minamarg Great Himalayan Pastures
  - **Route 3:** Tosamaidan & Doodhpathri Alpine Corridors (*Budgam*)
  - **Route 4:** Gurez & Tulail Alpine Grasslands via Razdan Pass (*Bandipora*)
* Real-time support points:
  - 🚑 **1962 Mobile Veterinary Clinics (Animal Ambulances)**
  - 💧 Mandatory Chemical Dipping Vats
  - 🌾 Emergency Fodder & Mineral Salt Depots
  - ✂️ Mobile Transit Shearing Camps
